Built for three audiences, one goal

One platform, three ways in.

Literacy improves fastest when the adults around a child are pulling in the same direction. Deleglow Academy meets each of them where they are.

For educators

Classroom-ready lesson plans, diagnostic tools, and a structured phonics-to-fluency framework you can adapt to your curriculum.

For parents

Simple, guided activities to build reading habits at home — no teaching background required, just fifteen minutes a day.

For global partners

Turnkey program materials, impact reporting, and a shared curriculum so NGOs and ministries can scale literacy work without starting from zero.

Why literacy, why now

Reading is the one skill every other subject depends on.

A child who falls behind on reading by age eight rarely catches up on their own. Deleglow Academy was built to close that window early — with a framework that's simple enough for a first-time volunteer and rigorous enough for a trained teacher.

We don't sell a single app or a single curriculum. We connect the people already working on this problem — teachers, parents, and program partners — with a shared, adaptable toolkit.
