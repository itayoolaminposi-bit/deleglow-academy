How it works

From sign-up to a running program in five steps.

The path looks slightly different for a classroom teacher, a parent, or a partner organization — but it follows the same five stages.

01

Tell us who you're supporting

Sign up and tell us whether you're an educator, a parent, or a partner organization, and roughly how many learners you're working with. This sets up the right starting materials.

02

Run the diagnostic

A short, guided check-in places each learner at the right point in the phonemic-awareness-to-comprehension sequence, so no one starts too far ahead or behind.

03

Get your session plan

You receive a matched set of lesson plans or home activities — classroom pacing guides for teachers, fifteen-minute routines for parents, facilitator scripts for partner programs.

04

Teach, track, adjust

Run sessions on your own schedule. Built-in checkpoints tell you when a learner is ready to move forward, and where to slow down and reinforce a skill.

05

See the progress add up

Simple progress views for individual learners, and program-level reporting for partners tracking outcomes across multiple sites.

What's different by audience

The same five steps, adapted to your setting.

Educator

Whole classroom, standards-aligned

Diagnostics for every student, group-level pacing, and reporting that maps to your existing gradebook.

Parent

One child, fifteen minutes a day

A single simplified diagnostic and a rotating set of short home activities — no lesson planning required.

Partner

Multi-site, volunteer-run

Facilitator scripts built for first-time volunteers, plus aggregated reporting across every site in the program.
