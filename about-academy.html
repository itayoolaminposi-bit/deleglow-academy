About Deleglow Academy

A reading framework, not another app to learn.

Deleglow Academy is the structured literacy framework at the center of everything we build — the lesson sequences, the diagnostic checks, and the home activities all come from the same core method.

The framework

Four pillars, built to reinforce each other.

Phonemic awareness

Hearing and manipulating the sounds inside words, before letters are even introduced.

Phonics & decoding

Connecting sounds to letters and letter patterns, with a clear sequence from simple to complex.

Fluency

Repeated, supported reading practice that builds speed and accuracy without losing comprehension.

Comprehension

Making sense of what's been read — vocabulary, background knowledge, and active discussion.

Why it holds up outside a classroom

Every lesson has one clear objective, one script, and one way to check it worked.

A parent or first-time volunteer doesn't need to interpret an abstract standard — they follow the same clear structure a trained teacher uses, adapted for a smaller setting.

Educators get the full framework with pacing guides and diagnostic tools; parents get a simplified version focused on daily habits; partners get both, plus the reporting layer to track progress across a program.

Who it's designed for

Ages 4 through 10, across three settings.

Classroom

Whole-class & small-group

Lesson sequences that slot into an existing school day, with built-in checkpoints for tracking each student.

Home

Daily fifteen-minute sessions

A simplified activity set parents can run without any teaching background, tied to the same skill sequence.

Program

Community & after-school

Volunteer-friendly session plans designed for NGOs and community programs running at scale.
