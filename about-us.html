About us

We're a small team that got tired of watching good literacy work stay small.

Deleglow Academy started as a shared framework between a handful of teachers and program leads who kept solving the same problem separately. We built the platform we wished we'd had.

Our mission

Put a working reading framework within reach of anyone teaching a child to read.

Whether that's a classroom teacher with thirty students, a parent at the kitchen table, or an NGO running a program across a dozen villages — the framework should be the same, and it should work without a specialist in the room.

Our vision

A world where a child's zip code doesn't decide whether they learn to read on time.

We measure ourselves by how far the framework travels — into new languages, new classrooms, and homes with no prior access to structured reading support — not by how polished any single tool looks.

What we value

Three things guide every decision we make.

Evidence over trend

Our framework is built on established reading-science fundamentals, not whatever teaching fad is popular this year. If it doesn't hold up in a real classroom, it doesn't ship.

Usable by non-specialists

A tool that only works in the hands of a trained reading specialist doesn't scale. Everything we build is tested with first-time volunteers and busy parents, not just teachers.

Built with partners, not for them

Our global partners shape the curriculum as much as they use it. Programs on the ground know their communities better than we ever will from a distance.
